Welcome to Mehendiganj

A charming town in southern Bangladesh

About Mehendiganj

Mehendiganj is a picturesque upazila in the Barisal District of Bangladesh. Surrounded by rivers and green landscapes, it's known for its natural beauty, hospitality, and rich cultural heritage.

Geography

Situated on the banks of the Meghna River, Mehendiganj is prone to seasonal flooding, but its fertile lands support vibrant agriculture and fishing communities.

Culture & Festivals

The people of Mehendiganj celebrate traditional Bengali festivals like Pohela Boishakh, Eid, and Durga Puja with great enthusiasm. Folk music and boat races are a part of local entertainment.
